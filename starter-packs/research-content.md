# Starter Pack: Research & Content

Use this pack for market research, decision support, editorial programs, reports, or evidence-backed content.

## Required inputs

- research question and intended decision;
- audience and publication context;
- approved internal sources;
- source-quality requirements;
- voice, legal, factual, and citation constraints.

## Workflow

1. define the research question and decision;
2. create a source plan and evidence standard;
3. collect and normalize evidence;
4. separate findings, interpretations, and unknowns;
5. validate one content template;
6. produce a pilot;
7. run the batch with sampling;
8. fact-check, edit, approve, and publish.

## Gates

- **Source Gate:** dynamic facts include source and access date.
- **Synthesis Gate:** evidence, inference, and opinion remain distinguishable.
- **Template Gate:** one pilot passes before scaling.
- **Editorial Gate:** claims, citations, originality, voice, and reader value are reviewed.

## Initialization prompt

```text
Initialize the Research & Content starter pack in Standard mode.
Do not draft final content yet.
Create a charter, research question, source hierarchy, evidence table,
assumption and contradiction register, pilot outline, editorial quality gate,
and measurable Definition of Done.
```
