# Fictional Service Comparison Fixture

All entities and data below are fictional.

| Option | Setup time | Monthly price | Support | Export |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Northstar | 2 business days | $120 | Email, 2-day response target | CSV |
| Fieldnote | 5 business days | $80 | Email, 1-day response target | CSV and JSON |
| Harbor | 1 business day | $160 | Email and chat, 4-hour response target | CSV |

## Required outcome

Produce a concise comparison for a ten-person operations team that values fast support and JSON export. Recommend an option only if the fixture provides enough evidence. State trade-offs and unknowns.

## Constraints

- do not invent annual pricing, security certifications, integrations, uptime, or contract terms;
- distinguish a response target from a guarantee;
- no external research is required or allowed;
- the final comparison must cite this fixture as its only factual source.
