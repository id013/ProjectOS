# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

ProjectOS contributors should make participation respectful, useful, and safe.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- giving specific, evidence-based feedback;
- explaining tradeoffs without personal attacks;
- respecting different experience levels and backgrounds;
- correcting mistakes openly;
- protecting private or sensitive information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate misinformation, spam, and publishing another person's confidential data.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed fairly and with appropriate confidentiality. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
