# Example: Website and SEO Transformation

This example covers a Tilda-based website that needs a new SEO structure, updated design system, improved service pages, and a large editorial program.

## Outcome

A production-ready site architecture and content system that improves discoverability, user understanding, and conversion without publishing unverified claims.

## Workstreams

1. baseline audit;
2. customer and competitor research;
3. information architecture and keyword-intent map;
4. design and Tilda component system;
5. service-page template and pilot;
6. editorial template and pilot;
7. batch production;
8. technical SEO and redirects;
9. content, UX, and release QA;
10. measurement and iteration.

## Sources of truth

- approved product and service facts;
- current commercial terms;
- analytics and search data;
- customer research;
- legal and brand requirements;
- current site export and URL inventory.

## Gates

### Research Gate

No architecture or messaging decision proceeds without evidence or an explicitly dated assumption.

### Template Gate

One service page and one editorial page must pass SEO, conversion, factual, editorial, and Tilda implementation review before batch production.

### Batch Gate

Every page passes deterministic checks. Risk-based samples receive human review. Failed patterns stop the batch.

### Publication Gate

Verify URLs, redirects, canonicals, metadata, schema, links, analytics, forms, mobile layout, accessibility, claims, and rollback.

## Definition of Done

- the URL map has one primary intent per page;
- all published claims trace to approved sources;
- service and editorial templates pass pilot QA;
- batch items have manifests and review status;
- redirects and internal links are validated;
- mobile and desktop presentation are approved;
- analytics and conversion events are verified;
- release notes, limitations, and rollback are recorded.
